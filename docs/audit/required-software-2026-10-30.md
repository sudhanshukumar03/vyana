# Required software follow-up - 30 October 2026

This implements the sensor-free priorities from the [scope audit](scope-cleanup-2026-10-30.md). It does not claim that compiled firmware is installed, a usable body-connected ECG was acquired, or real Google activity was imported.

## Implemented

- **Google-only activity:** product/API/AI summaries no longer fall back to device counters. Legacy ingestion/history remains owned and readable for compatibility, but the product stops requesting that history. Google zero and distance-only records remain distinct from missing data. Cards, state and loaded exports share Google day semantics. Disconnect/sync revision guards cover activity totals as well as the connection cache, preventing delayed snapshots from restoring disconnected totals.
- **ECG capture and delivery:** the AD8232 sample hook feeds a separate 512-sample ring. Batches contain up to 128 exact raw ADC/lead samples, per-sample time offsets, a boot UUID and first-sample sequence. Health summaries retain priority. Retries preserve identity; a pending waveform batch expires after 30 seconds. Buffer overflow/restart remains visible through sequence gaps. This is volatile best-effort capture, not lossless or guaranteed 250 Hz recording.
- **ECG API/storage:** authenticated device ingestion validates source, size, values, time and strictly increasing offsets. Identical retries deduplicate; changed/overlapping sequences return 409. Reads are scoped atomically to the active owned device, capped at 24 batches. Server retention is ten minutes and at most 1,200 batches/device, with a cleanup worker in the normal API process. Existing full-vital and partial-snapshot history are separate.
- **Recorded waveform UI:** Dashboard/Vitals can poll raw sample batches independently of complete vitals or equipment summaries. The chart shows the latest four seconds, capped at 1,000 samples; it never fills gaps for missing ordinals, reboot, lead-off or intervals over 20 ms. Pause/resume, exact loaded-sample CSV and an accessible values table are provided. The generated demo remains explicitly separate. Raw ADC is not a calibrated clinical ECG, rhythm classifier or diagnostic result.
- **Unsupported controls:** the initial Activity cleanup replaced unavailable calories/active time with recorded days/last sync; the subsequent Google metrics extension below supersedes that change. Settings presents metric/light support without inert preference buttons. The unsupported trauma filter is removed; provider-listed clinics remain filterable. Prototype score panels remain labelled/unavailable when their required input is missing or unclassified.
- **Location provider:** a real request exposed the unsupported category `healthcare.clinic`. The proxy now uses the documented `healthcare.clinic_or_praxis` category ([Geoapify documentation](https://apidocs.geoapify.com/docs/places/)). Real local API checks returned HTTP 200 for New Delhi geocoding and 25 facility listings with valid coordinates. This verifies access/query operation, not facility accuracy, emergency capability, current opening hours or telephone availability. The temporary synthetic account and its local session/token records were removed.
- **Operating instructions:** current feature/API/hardware guides and the [exhibition procedure](../DEMO.md) describe Google activity, sample transport, retention, startup and limits.
